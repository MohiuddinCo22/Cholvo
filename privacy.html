Cholvo Privacy Policy

DRAFT. This is a starting text for the Cholvo Rider App. Have it reviewed and edited by Cholvo management (and a lawyer) before launch.

What we collect from riders

Name, photo, mobile number, email, date of birth, addresses, identity document details and pictures (NID, passport or birth certificate), utility bill, vehicle details, nominee details, and your live location while you are online in the app.

Why we collect it

To check your identity, approve your rider account, assign deliveries, show customers where their order is, keep delivery records and cash accounts, and keep Cholvo, merchants and customers safe.

Who can see it

Only authorised Cholvo staff see your documents. Merchants and customers see only your name, phone number and live position during their own delivery. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

While your account is active and as long as the law or our accounts require. You can ask us to delete your account and papers by contacting Cholvo support.

Contact

Cholvo support: use the Help Center in the Rider App.
